Assess and certify the skills the future will need.

Teraminds Talent AI builds AI agents that evaluate real capability in new-age and emerging skills, then certify it, so employers can hire, upskill and deploy people with evidence instead of guesswork.

Talk to usHow it works

The problem

Skills are changing faster than resumes, degrees and interviews can keep up. New roles in AI, semiconductors, green energy and advanced manufacturing have no trusted way to verify who can actually do the work.

Credentials lag reality

Degrees and certificates rarely reflect emerging, hands-on skills that employers need today.

Hiring is slow and subjective

Interview panels vary, and assessing technical depth at scale costs HR teams time and money.

Upskilling is hard to prove

Companies invest in training but struggle to measure what people can now do on the job.

How it works

An AI-first assessment and certification workflow, designed from three decades of HR and talent-management practice.

01

Map the skill

We break a role into observable competencies, built with employers and domain experts.

02

Assess with AI agents

Adaptive, scenario-based assessments and AI-led interviews test applied ability, not memorised answers.

03

Certify and verify

Candidates earn a verifiable certificate; employers get a clear, comparable skills profile.

04

Guide what's next

Gap reports turn results into targeted learning paths for individuals and teams.

Who it's for

We start where skill shortages are sharpest and the cost of a wrong hire is highest.

  • Employers and HR teams hiring or redeploying talent into emerging roles.
  • Semiconductor and electronics manufacturing: certifying fab and OSAT technicians for India's new chip units.
  • Training providers and institutions that want industry-recognised proof of outcomes.
  • Professionals who want credible evidence of new skills to move ahead.
