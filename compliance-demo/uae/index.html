WPS due dateLast month's wages reach the worker by the 1st of the next Gregorian month. Later is a delay.Ministerial Resolution No. 0340 of 2026, Art. 1 (from 1 Jun 2026)


WPS 85% thresholdEstablishment compliant if ≥85% of total wages due are transferred on time; a worker counts as paid at ≥85% with a lawful reason for the rest. The balance is still owed.MR 0340/2026; MoHRE news 4 Jun 2026


GratuityForeign full-time workers with ≥1 year: 21 days' basic wage per year for years 1–5, 30 days after, pro-rata for part years, capped at 2 years' wage; unpaid absence excluded. Daily wage = basic ÷ 30 (common practice). Emiratis are covered by pension law instead.Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021, Art. 51


Final settlementGratuity and dues paid to a leaver within 14 days of the last day.FDL 33/2021, Art. 53


Emiratisation (50+ staff)+2% Emiratis in skilled roles per year (1% by 30 Jun, 1% by 31 Dec 2026). Shortfall priced at AED 10,000 per missing role per month (H1 2026 rate).MoHRE, 7 May and 22 Jun 2026


Emiratisation (20–49 staff)Firms in 14 named sectors must employ at least one UAE national.MoHRE Emiratisation targets guidance
